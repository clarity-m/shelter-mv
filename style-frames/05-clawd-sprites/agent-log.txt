I've finished the Clawd model sheet (`sprites.png`), the evolution ladder (`ladder.png`), the sprite data file (`clawd-sprites.js`) and `NOTES.md`. The sheet has 14 emotes, each also shown at 3×6 px, plus walk, hop, sit, sit-on-a-ledge, wave, carry, hold, sleep and look-up strips and a kit of eye shapes. Every sprite stays on the strict 1:2 terminal grid, and converting the canonical grid back to terminal characters reproduces the Claude Code glyph exactly, which confirms the decode is right. Emotes use only the two eye holes, the arm tips, the legs and one-pixel shifts. Arch- and cup-shaped eyes carry happy and content without adding a mouth, and the ladder's final starburst uses Clawd's limbs as its 11 rays, which gives the opening and closing a real morph. The weak spots are the look-up pose, which works in motion but not as a still, chunky "!" and z accents, and leg beams on rung 5 that look a bit like spurs; sprites cost about 0.05 ms each to draw, but the ladder's glowing rungs take about 1 s on the CPU and would need to move to GPU shaders to fit the 3–5 s frame budget.
I've added the extruded 3D Clawd sheet as three new files: `clawd-3d.png`, `clawd-3d.html` and `clawd-3d.js`. It shows front, 3/4, side, back and top views under a warm key and a cool fill, plus blink, happy, sad, surprised, sleepy and love in 3/4 beside their 2D sprites. The motion keys are hop apex (lifted clear of its ground shadow) and walk passing, and there's a small depth 3/4/5 comparison. `Clawd3D.buildVoxels(grid, depth, opts)` extrudes any sprite into a list of boxes; I ran it on every emote and motion frame, at depths 3–5 and in punched-eye mode, and all came out valid. `clawd-sprites.js` is unchanged, and `NOTES.md` has a new round-3 section. On this laptop the 16 views take 6.3 s to render, so one Clawd about 300 px wide costs roughly 0.1 s; crowds should draw the box list as instanced cubes instead. Weak spots: the side and top views are flat slabs by nature, the eyes turn orange and stop reading as eyes if the pockets are deeper than 1.0, and punched-through eyes only show when seen from almost straight in front or behind.
