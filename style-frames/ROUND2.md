
## Round 2 notes (read these before your frame section)

Round 1 made four frames in sibling folders: 01-pretrain, 02-environment, 03-hand, 04-swarm. Each has frame.png, its source, and NOTES.md. You may READ them for reference, but never write there. The director's feedback:

- 01-pretrain's visual clarity is the ideal for the whole video: one clear focal point, generous negative space, a restrained palette, instantly readable. Aim for that clarity.
- Low contrast is a flaw. 04 was too hazy and flat. Use the full value range.
- Mixing geometry languages is a flaw. 02's blocky voxel foreground clashed with its smooth polygonal background.
- Heavy stylization is welcome. A committed style makes imperfect anatomy irrelevant, e.g. risograph/halftone print with 2-3 inks, bold outlines and slight misregistration. Commit fully to your style's conventions instead of hedging toward realism.
- Clawd stays the pixel glyph in every style: 18x5, pixels 1:2, #D97757.
- Render budget matters: production needs about 3-5 s per 1080p frame. Note your frame's render time in NOTES.md and how you would hit that budget.
