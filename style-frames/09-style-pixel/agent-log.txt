I finished the pixel-art style frame of the harness scene. It's a night lab at 480x270, scaled 4x to 1920x1080, with 30 colors, and I checked that every native pixel is an exact 4x4 block. Clawd glows inside jointed ribs that curl up like two cupped hands, one researcher leans in with a stylus with her face lit only by him, and the other stands back with a mug watching a gray gridworld and an eval curve at 0.62. It reads in about a second, Clawd is clearly the focal point, and his light is the only saturated color, so "color = capability" comes through directly. The weak spot is the humans. They're charming at about 25 px, but the hands are 8-pixel mittens and the second researcher is stiff, so tender close-ups would need a real pixel artist; the empty cabinet in the lower third is dead space too. A frame takes about 0.3–0.5 s to draw and about 4 s including Chrome startup, which already fits the 3–5 s budget; I think this style fits the fidelity-dial idea best of the four, and I'd use it for the early acts rather than the whole video.
