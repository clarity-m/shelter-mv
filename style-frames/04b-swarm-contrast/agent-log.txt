04b is finished. `frame.png` shows near-black space, a blown-white Alpha Centauri B cupped by five thin bands of about 380k crisp one-pixel collectors, and 11 shafts of light that appear beyond the bands to form the spark. I kept the cloud bank, recast as the edge of the humans' sim that the camera has just left: a crisp soap-film boundary with the painted golden-hour sky inside. `alt-nosim.png` shows the other option, with the edge removed, for comparison. The frame is one deterministic WebGL2 file (re-renders are byte-identical) that draws in about 0.15 s after about 2 s of one-time shader compile, so 3–5 s per frame is easy if frames are rendered in one page. Contrast, clarity and the spark are fixed, but at thumbnail size the bands still read more as fine dust rings than as millions of instances, and in a still the sim edge could pass for a pastel planet until the pull-back gives it context.

The swarm is now a 3D cage of eight crossing orbital bands: nearer collectors are brighter and larger, two nearly edge-on bands cross the star as a dark X, and the far arcs are dim red threads that disappear behind the disk.
