The pretraining frame is done: a quiet gray, Sugimoto-like seascape where the horizon is the token stream, a thin line of light made of tiny letter-like glyphs with motion trails. One token has stopped and become Clawd, crisp in exact #D97757 and backlit by an irregular 11-ray burst, and it is the only saturated thing in the frame. Faint attention arcs reach toward it from earlier tokens, their dim reflection makes a cupped bowl in the floor, and the floor is a faint weight-matrix heatmap in perspective, with the step/loss HUD in the bottom-left corner.

**How it went:** it took 13 render-and-critique passes. I cut two ideas: pixel fragments around Clawd looked like it was shattering, and an orange trail looked like a wire.

**Weak spots:** I showed the moment the burst becomes Clawd rather than the full square → burst → Clawd morph. The upper third is deliberately empty and may feel too austere. The brushwork is barely visible at full size.

**Strength:** it reads at a glance, and all the parts of the video's visual grammar come through without any on-screen text.
