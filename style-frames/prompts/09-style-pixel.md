## YOUR FRAME. Your folder: 09-style-pixel

STYLE: high-end pixel art, in the lineage of Eastward, Hyper Light Drifter and Owlboy. Required features:
- The whole frame authored at a low native resolution: 480x270, scaled 4x nearest-neighbor to 1920x1080.
- A limited palette of about 16-32 colors, with deliberate dithering and hand-placed-looking clusters.
- Lighting done through palette ramps and pixel-art light shafts.
- No blurry filtering and no mixed pixel sizes. Clawd native at this resolution is the one exception: he may be at his canonical grid at a larger scale, as long as his pixels stay crisp and integer-aligned. Decide what reads best and explain.

The conceptual appeal: Clawd is natively pixel, and pixel resolution can become the fidelity dial across the video (the world's resolution rises as capability grows). Say in NOTES.md how that dial could work.
