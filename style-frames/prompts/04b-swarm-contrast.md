## YOUR FRAME. Your folder: 04b-swarm-contrast

A REVISION of 04-swarm. Start by copying ../04-swarm/frame.html into your folder, then read ../04-swarm/NOTES.md.

The director's verdict: too low contrast, and at a glance the swarm reads as a glowing ball of yarn or an armillary sphere, not as millions of instances. Changes:
- Full value range: near-black deep space, a star core blown to white with an orange falloff, and crisp bright collectors.
- The swarm as a myriad of tiny crisp points in a few THIN inclined bands (not thick ribbons), with real dark gaps between the bands. Density and fineness should sell "millions" even at thumbnail size.
- Keep 11 lanes of escaping light forming the spark, but it must now be clearly visible as bright shafts against black: still elegant, discoverable in a second, not a pasted logo.
- The cloud bank: either remove it, or make it unmistakably the edge of the simulation's sky the camera has just exited (for example a curved luminous boundary with the painted sky inside). Your call; justify it in NOTES.md.
- Keep Alpha Centauri A as the second, yellow-white star, and keep the EP 000001 / STEP 000000 HUD.
- Aim for the clarity of 01-pretrain: one focal point and generous negative space.
