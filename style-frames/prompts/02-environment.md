## YOUR FRAME. Your folder: 02-environment

"Environment showcase": an instrumental drop, mid-arc (roughly 40-60% saturation). This is where the video flexes environment design.

A training environment starting to approximate the real world: for example, procedural terrain built from gridworld tiles or voxels that transitions into naturalistic landforms toward the horizon, under a Shinkai-like golden-hour sky, with a scattering of geometric agents (capsules, cubes, MuJoCo-style stick walkers) mid-task. One pixel Clawd among them.

Color-as-capability must read at a glance: the world is gray where untouched and saturated where Clawd's learning has reached, e.g. a region of color blooming outward from Clawd across the tiles. Subtle environment scaffolding (bounding walls, a faint grid, a reset boundary) and a sparse HUD (episode number, reward). Rich, painterly, luminous: it should make someone want to live there.
