## YOUR FRAME. Your folder: 07-style-ligne-claire

STYLE: ligne claire, the clean-line comics tradition of Herge and Moebius. Required features:
- Uniform confident linework.
- Flat color fields with no gradients, or at most one flat shadow tone.
- Precise architectural and technical detail on the harness, lab gear and monitors.
- Crisp, calm, maximally legible.

Moebius-style muted flat palettes suit the early phase. The appeal of this style is total clarity and elegance. It should feel like a beautifully drawn plate from a French graphic novel about building a mind.
