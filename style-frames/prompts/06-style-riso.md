## YOUR FRAME. Your folder: 06-style-riso

STYLE: risograph / halftone print. Use 2-3 spot inks (for example a warm gray or black, a fluorescent orange near #D97757 for Clawd, and optionally one accent such as fluoro pink or a federal blue). Required features:
- Real halftone dot screens at angles.
- Overprint blending: inks multiply.
- Slight layer misregistration.
- Bold confident outlines.
- Paper texture and grain, with ink density variation and a little roughness.

Reference vibe: indie riso zines and screenprinted posters: punchy, graphic, forgiving of anatomy. Print can't glow, so any sense of light comes from ink density and paper white. Clawd can be printed in the orange ink but must keep his crisp pixel grid.
