
## YOUR SHOT: fusion ignition (S21, verse 2, 2:14.8)

In the middle of the film, a single kick drum hit on bar 57 ignites a fusion
reactor. This is the key image of Claude's work arriving in the real world. There
is no Clawd and there are no humans in it; the impact shows only as light.

The frame is the moment just after ignition. A tokamak sits in a dark, cavernous
hall. In layered paper, the torus seen at a three-quarter angle is natural:
- the front arc of D-shaped field coils sits on a near layer;
- the plasma ring is a glowing vellum layer behind it;
- the back arc of coils sits on a layer behind the plasma.

The coils repeat around the ring. Orange plasma light escapes through the gaps
between them and through small diagnostic ports. It throws long radial shafts
through the hall's haze, fans of light on the floor, and patterned light on the
walls, the way a punched lantern throws its pattern. The hall's structure is
layered deep-indigo silhouettes: gantries, catwalk railings, cable trays, the
bulk of the cryostat. It is rim-lit orange where the light reaches. A few tiny
cool indicator pinholes sit elsewhere. The torus is the film's cupped shape again:
a ring holding a fire.

Warm light covers perhaps a third of the frame. Keep the rest dark and cool. Aim
for awe, but tender: this is a sunrise, not a weapon.

Stretch goal, only after the still is strong: render a 3 s (90-frame, 30 fps)
ignition test to `ignite.mp4` with ffmpeg. The plasma goes from dark to lit on
frame 10 (the kick), the shafts sweep out, and the camera pushes in slowly so the
paper layers show parallax.
