## YOUR FRAME. Your folder: 08-style-anime-cel

STYLE: 2D anime cel, the look of the original Shelter MV and anime film generally. Required features:
- Characters in cel style: clean colored or dark lines, flat base colors with one hard-edged shadow tone and occasional highlight shapes. This is 2D drawing, NOT 3D toon shading.
- A painted anime background behind them, softer and more rendered than the characters.
- Cinematic anime lighting: rim light, a light shaft, dust motes.

Keep the early-phase palette restrained (desaturated room, orange Clawd, maybe a cool monitor glow). The appeal is emotional warmth and the direct lineage to Shelter.
