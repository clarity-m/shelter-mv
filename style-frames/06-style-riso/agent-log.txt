The riso frame is finished in `06-style-riso`: a night-lab print in black, riso Midnight (a slate gray-blue) and Claude orange. Tiny Clawd glows on a workbench inside a harness of jointed ribs that reads as two cupped hands; one researcher leans in with a stylus while the other watches a gray gridworld on a monitor. Clawd prints exactly #D97757 with crisp edges, the values run from paper white to near-black, and the frame renders in about 3 s in the page (about 10 s including Chrome startup). On whether humans hold up: faces in profile do (glasses, blush and a soft smile survive the 2.2× close-up in `detail.png`), but hands are simplified mittens that work at mid-shot and would need hand-drawn poses for close-ups. I chose Midnight over the prettier federal blue so the early world stays desaturated (`ink-compare.png` shows three inks side by side), and the biggest open risk is how the 7 px halftone dots survive motion and video compression.
